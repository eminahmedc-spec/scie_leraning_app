🏷️Nouns and Verbs

A noun names a person, place, or thing. A verb is an action word — something you can do.

The dog (noun) runs (verb).

✏️Spelling and Plurals

For most words, add -s to make it plural. After sh, ch, s, or x, add -es instead.

cat → cats, box → boxes

📖Making Sentences

Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. It needs a who and a what.

The sun is bright.
