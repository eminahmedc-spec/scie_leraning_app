🌱Living Things

Plants and animals are alive. They need food, water, and air — and they grow.

A seed + sunlight + water → a plant 🌱

🪐The Solar System

Eight planets travel around the Sun. Mercury is closest to the Sun, and Earth is our home.

Sun → Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → …

🧊Solids, Liquids and Gases

A solid keeps its shape, a liquid flows, and a gas fills all the space it can.

ice (solid) → water (liquid) → steam (gas)
