Quick lessons

➕Adding and Taking Away

Start with the bigger number and count on. For subtraction, count backwards instead.

8 + 5 → start at 8, count on: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13

✖️Multiply Means Groups

Multiplying is a fast way to add equal groups. Three groups of four is easier as one times sum.

4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so 4 × 3 = 12

🔷Shapes and Halves

A triangle has 3 sides, a square has 4, and a circle has none. A half is 2 equal parts.

Half of 10 → 10 splits into 5 and 5
